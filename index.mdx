Kiosco is an all-in-one virtual store that runs entirely in the browser. It gives small businesses a ready-to-use product catalog, shopping cart, order management system, and admin dashboard — with no backend infrastructure needed. Everything is stored locally and works offline as a Progressive Web App.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the store">
    Open `index.html` in a browser or deploy the files to any static hosting provider. No build step required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the admin panel">
    Click the **Admin** button in the top-right corner and enter the registered phone number to log in. The default admin number is `+51914491874`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your products">
    Go to the **Dashboard** tab, create categories under **Categorías**, then add products with name, price, stock, and cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your business">
    Open **Configuración** to set your business name, RUC, delivery cost, free-delivery threshold, and accepted payment methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your store">
    Share the URL with customers. They browse your catalog, add to cart, and place orders — you manage everything from the admin panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Kiosco works offline once loaded. Install it as a PWA on your phone or tablet to access the admin panel on the go without an internet connection.
</Tip>
