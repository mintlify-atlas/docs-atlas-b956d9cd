---
title: "Manage products and categories in Kiosco"
description: "Add, edit, and organize your product catalog into categories with images, pricing, stock tracking, and cost data for profit analytics in Kiosco."
---

Kiosco organizes your product catalog as a flat object where each key is a category name and each value is an array of products. You manage everything from the **Dashboard** tab of the admin panel, which gives you separate sub-tabs for products and categories.

## Data structure

Products are stored in `localStorage` under the key `kiosco_products` in the following shape:

```json kiosco_products
{
  "🥤 Bebidas": [
    { "id": 1, "name": "Coca Cola 500ml", "price": 3.5, "stock": 50, "cost": 2.0 }
  ],
  "🍿 Snacks": [
    { "id": 5, "name": "Papas Lays", "price": 4.0, "stock": 20, "cost": 2.5 }
  ],
  "🍫 Dulces": [
    { "id": 8, "name": "Chocolate Sublime", "price": 2.5, "stock": 40, "cost": 1.2 }
  ]
}
```

The three default categories ship with nine sample products. You can rename, extend, or replace them entirely.

## Product fields

Each product record contains the following fields:

<ParamField path="id" type="number" required>
  Auto-generated unique identifier using `Date.now()`. Used to link product images and track sales across orders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  The product name shown on the storefront and in invoices.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="price" type="number" required>
  The customer-facing selling price in Soles (S/). This is what appears on the storefront and what gets charged at checkout.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="stock" type="number" default="0">
  The current inventory count. Products with zero stock remain visible but cannot be added to the cart.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="cost" type="number">
  Your purchase cost in Soles (S/). Not shown to customers. Used exclusively for profit calculations in the Reports tab. If omitted, the system estimates cost as 60% of the selling price.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  `price` and `cost` serve different purposes. `price` is what customers pay; `cost` is what you paid to acquire the product. The difference is your gross profit per unit, which feeds the **Most Profitable Products** report.
</Note>

## Managing categories

Categories must exist before you can add products to them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Categories tab">
    In the admin panel, go to **Dashboard** and click the **Categorías** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a category name">
    Type the category name in the input field. You can include an emoji prefix (e.g., `🧃 Jugos`) — it will appear as-is on the storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Agregar">
    Click the **Agregar** button. The category is saved immediately and becomes available in the product form's category selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete a category, click the trash icon next to it. Deleting a category also permanently removes all products in it.

<Warning>
  Deleting a category is irreversible. All products inside that category and their associated images will be removed from `localStorage`.
</Warning>

## Adding a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products tab">
    In the admin panel, go to **Dashboard** and click the **Productos** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category">
    Choose the target category from the dropdown at the top of the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the product fields">
    Enter the product name, selling price (S/), stock count, and optionally the cost (S/). All monetary values accept decimal increments of 0.50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Agregar">
    Click the **Agregar** button. The product appears in the list below, grouped under its category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing products

Once a product is added, you can update its stock and cost directly from the product list. Full name and price editing is not exposed in the UI — to change a product's name or price, delete it and re-add it with the correct values.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Updating stock">
    Click the **Stock** button next to any product. A prompt shows the current stock count and lets you enter a new value. The storefront reflects the change immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Updating cost">
    Click the **Costo** button next to any product. Enter the new purchase cost in Soles. This only affects profit analytics — customers never see this value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deleting a product">
    Click the trash icon next to any product and confirm the deletion. The product and all its associated images are removed from storage.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Adding product images

Each product can have multiple images. Images are stored as base64 data URLs in `localStorage` under the key `kiosco_product_images`, keyed by product ID.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the image gallery">
    Click the **Imágenes** button next to a product. A modal opens showing any existing images for that product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a file">
    Use the file input in the modal to choose an image from your device. A preview appears before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Agregar imagen">
    Click **Agregar imagen** to save the image. It is immediately linked to the product and displayed on the storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove images if needed">
    Each thumbnail in the gallery has an **✕** button. Click it to delete that individual image.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The first image in a product's gallery is used as its thumbnail on the storefront product cards. Upload your best-quality image first.
</Tip>

## Stock and the storefront

Stock levels directly control what customers can add to their cart. When a product's stock reaches zero, the add-to-cart button is disabled on the storefront, preventing overselling. The stock count decreases automatically when an order is placed and increases if an order is cancelled or deleted.
