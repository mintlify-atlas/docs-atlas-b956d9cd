---
title: "Configure your Kiosco business settings"
description: "Set your business name, phone number, RUC, address, delivery fees, and operating hours from the Kiosco admin panel Configuración tab."
---

The Settings tab in the Kiosco admin panel lets you control how your business appears to customers and how delivery costs are calculated. All settings are saved instantly to `localStorage` under the key `kiosco_settings` and take effect across the storefront and generated invoices without a page reload.

## Accessing settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin panel">
    Navigate to `admin.html` in your browser and log in with your registered phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Configuración tab">
    Click the **Configuración** tab in the main navigation bar at the top of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Update any fields and click **Guardar configuración** to persist your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Business identity

These fields identify your business on customer-facing pages and on generated invoices.

<ParamField path="businessName" type="string" default="Kiosco">
  The display name of your store. Appears in the storefront header, browser title, and on every invoice. Changing this value updates all new invoices immediately.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="businessPhone" type="string" default="+51914491874">
  Your contact phone number. Shown to customers and used as the default number in payment method instructions for Yape and Plin.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="businessRUC" type="string" default="">
  Your tax identification number (RUC). Printed on electronic invoices. Leave blank if you do not issue formal invoices.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="businessAddress" type="string" default="">
  Your business address. Printed on electronic invoices alongside the RUC.
</ParamField>

<Tip>
  Updating `businessName` affects the store header and all invoices generated after the change. Invoices already created retain the name that was active at the time they were issued.
</Tip>

## Delivery cost settings

Kiosco applies a flat delivery fee to every order unless the cart total reaches the free-delivery threshold.

<ParamField path="deliveryCost" type="number" default="3.0">
  The flat delivery fee charged per order, in Soles (S/). Displayed at checkout and included in the order total.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="freeDeliveryMin" type="number" default="20">
  The minimum cart subtotal (S/) required for free delivery. When a customer's cart reaches this amount, the delivery fee is waived automatically at checkout.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  Delivery cost settings are stored as part of `kiosco_settings` in `localStorage`. To configure delivery time windows, see [Delivery options](/setup/delivery).
</Note>

## Operating hours

<ParamField path="scheduleStart" type="string" default="08:00">
  The time your store opens, in 24-hour format. Used to determine when customers can place orders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="scheduleEnd" type="string" default="22:00">
  The time your store closes, in 24-hour format.
</ParamField>

## How settings are stored

All business settings are serialized as a single JSON object and saved to `localStorage` under the key `kiosco_settings`. The full default structure is:

```json kiosco_settings
{
  "businessName": "Kiosco",
  "businessPhone": "+51914491874",
  "businessRUC": "",
  "businessAddress": "",
  "deliveryCost": 3.0,
  "freeDeliveryMin": 20,
  "scheduleStart": "08:00",
  "scheduleEnd": "22:00",
  "paymentMethods": ["yape", "plin", "transferencia", "efectivo"],
  "deliveryWindows": ["10:00-12:00", "12:00-14:00", "14:00-16:00", "16:00-18:00", "18:00-20:00"]
}
```

Settings are loaded by `getSettings()` and written by `saveSettings()` in `database.js`. A `settingsUpdated` event is dispatched on `window` whenever settings are saved, so other parts of the application can react in real time.

<Warning>
  Because all data is stored in the browser's `localStorage`, settings are tied to the device and browser where the admin panel is running. Clearing browser storage will reset all settings to their defaults on next load.
</Warning>
